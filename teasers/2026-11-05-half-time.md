## Problem
The probability of having accidents on a road in one hour is $3/4$. What is the probability of accidents in half an hour?

## Solution
**The probability is $\tfrac12$.**

Assume accidents happen independently over time at a steady rate, so the two halves of the hour are independent and alike. Let $p$ be the probability of an accident in half an hour.

There is no accident in the hour exactly when there is none in either half:
$$
(1-p)^2 = 1 - \tfrac34 = \tfrac14.
$$
So $1 - p = \tfrac12$, and $p = \tfrac12$.

The tempting answer $\tfrac38$ (half of $\tfrac34$) is wrong: probabilities of "at least one" don't scale with time. The quantity that does is the probability of *none*, which multiplies.
