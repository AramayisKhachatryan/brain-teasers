## Problem
On pressing a button, a random number is generated uniformly between 0 & 1. You keep on generating these numbers until their sum exceeds 1. What is the probability that you need to press the button more than n times? What is the expected number of times you need to press the button?

## Solution
**$P(\text{more than } n \text{ presses}) = \dfrac{1}{n!}$, and the expected number of presses is $e \approx 2.718$.**

Call the numbers $U_1, U_2, \dots$ and let $N$ be the number of presses. You need more than $n$ presses exactly when the first $n$ numbers haven't passed 1 yet:
$$
P(N > n) = P(U_1 + \dots + U_n \le 1).
$$
The points $(U_1, \dots, U_n)$ with all $U_i \ge 0$ and sum at most 1 form a corner of the unit cube, a simplex of volume $\tfrac{1}{n!}$. (For $n = 2$ it's a triangle of area $\tfrac12$; for $n = 3$ a pyramid of volume $\tfrac16$; in general, cutting at height $t$ and integrating gives $\int_0^1 \frac{(1-t)^{n-1}}{(n-1)!}\,dt = \frac{1}{n!}$.) So
$$
P(N > n) = \frac{1}{n!}.
$$

For the expectation, use $E[N] = \sum_{n \ge 0} P(N > n)$ (true for any count $N \ge 0$):
$$
E[N] = \frac{1}{0!} + \frac{1}{1!} + \frac{1}{2!} + \dots = e.
$$
The first two terms are 1 each, because you always need at least 2 presses.
