## Problem
You roll a fair six-sided die again and again until the first 6 comes up, then stop.

Someone watched the whole thing and tells you one fact: **every roll you made was even** (only 2s and 4s, then the final 6).

Let $N$ be the number of rolls. What is $\mathbb{E}[N \mid \text{all rolls were even}]$?
