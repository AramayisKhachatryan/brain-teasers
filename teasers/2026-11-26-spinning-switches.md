## Problem
Four identical, unlabeled switches are wired in series to a light bulb. The switches are simple buttons whose state cannot be directly observed, but can be changed by pushing; they are mounted on the corners of a rotatable square. At any point, you may push, simultaneously, any subset of the buttons, but then an adversary spins the square. (The bulb lights only when all four switches are on, and you see the bulb at once.) Is there an algorithm that will enable you to turn on the bulb in at most a fixed number of spins?
