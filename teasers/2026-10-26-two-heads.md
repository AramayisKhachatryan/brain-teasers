## Problem
You repeatedly toss a fair coin until you observe two consecutive heads. What is the expected number of tosses?

## Solution
**6.** Condition on the first tosses. Let $E$ be the expected number of tosses.

- **HH** (probability $\tfrac14$): done in 2 tosses.
- **T** first (probability $\tfrac12$): 1 toss wasted, start over, so $1 + E$.
- **HT** (probability $\tfrac14$): 2 tosses wasted, start over, so $2 + E$.

$$
E = \tfrac14 \cdot 2 + \tfrac12 (1 + E) + \tfrac14 (2 + E) = \tfrac32 + \tfrac34 E
$$

So $\tfrac14 E = \tfrac32$ and $E = 6$.
