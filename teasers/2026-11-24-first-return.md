## Problem
You start at $0$ on the number line. At every step you move one unit to the left or one unit to the right, each with probability $\tfrac12$, independently of the past.

1. What is the probability that you come back to $0$ for the *first* time at step 4?
2. What is the probability that you don't come back to $0$ at all during the first 4 steps?
